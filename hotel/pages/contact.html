Frequently asked questions

Find quick answers

Find quick answers to some of the questions our guests ask most often.

How can I modify my reservation? ↓

You can contact our reservations team with your reservation number and requested changes. Our team will confirm availability and any applicable fees.

Do you offer airport transfers? ↓

Yes. Selected Maison Élan properties offer private airport transfers that can be arranged before arrival.

What time is check-in and check-out? ↓

Standard check-in begins at 2:00 PM and check-out is before 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request.

Can I make a special request? ↓

Absolutely. You can include special requests during booking or contact our guest relations team before your arrival.

Can I cancel my reservation? ↓

Cancellation policies vary depending on the room and rate selected. Please review the cancellation terms included with your reservation.

Do you accommodate special celebrations? ↓

Yes. Our teams can arrange personalized experiences for birthdays, anniversaries, honeymoons, weddings, and other special occasions.
