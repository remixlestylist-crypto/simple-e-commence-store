About Azure Pearl Resort

Azure Pearl Resort is an exclusive island retreat designed for travelers seeking privacy, exceptional service, and a deeper connection with the natural beauty of the Maldives.

Each private villa is thoughtfully positioned to frame uninterrupted ocean views, offering an intimate sense of seclusion without compromising on comfort. Personalized service is at the heart of every stay.

The resort's fine dining venues showcase fresh, locally inspired cuisine, while the wellness sanctuary and ocean experiences create an unforgettable escape.
